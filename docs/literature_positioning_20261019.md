# Literature positioning and claim limits — 19 October 2026

This is a targeted update, not a completed systematic novelty search. Publisher pages and indexed primary-paper records were searched for Wenchong Tian, Zeda Yin, interpretable drainage RL, safe recurrent RL, knowledge fusion and neurosymbolic CSO control. Several full publisher pages returned HTTP 403; available primary-paper previews were used only for the claims below. No absence of a method from a preview is treated as evidence that the paper lacks it. Scopus and Web of Science coverage has not been verified.

Tian et al., *Improving the interpretability of deep reinforcement learning in urban drainage system operation*, **Water Research 249 (2024), 120912**, is directly relevant. Interpretability of drainage RL is already a research topic, so merely adding explanations cannot establish novelty. Verified identifiers: [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38042066/) and [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.watres.2023.120912). Detailed rainfall splits and matched performance comparisons must be extracted from the full study before making a quantitative comparison.

Yin et al., *Forecasting and optimization for minimizing combined sewer overflows using Machine learning frameworks and its inversion techniques*, is in **Journal of Hydrology 628 (2024), 130515**, rather than Water Resources Research. The publisher describes CSO forecasting and inversion-based optimization; its reported forecasting accuracy is not interchangeable with this project's control-performance metrics. [Publisher record](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0022169423014579), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jhydrol.2023.130515), [NSF manuscript location](https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10523713). The manuscript location was identified but could not be retrieved in this update.

The primary publisher preview for *Real-time control of urban drainage system for flood and combined sewer overflow mitigation with a novel recurrent deep reinforcement learning framework* describes **Safe DRQN**, safety-constrained DQN comparisons under partial observability, and decision-tree interpretation. It identifies multiple-seed training-variability analysis and an MPC comparison as future work. Thus safety constraints plus explanations are also insufficient as a broad novelty claim. These statements are limited to the retrieved preview; numerical superiority is not established. [Publisher preview](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0043135426004793).

The search additionally surfaced *Toward the full utilization of storage capacity: Urban drainage system operation integrating deep reinforcement learning and green infrastructure*. Its publisher preview describes a GI cooperative index incorporated into DRL rewards to encourage use of storage margins. The page labels the issue January 2027, so its online-publication timing relative to this audit needs verification before citing it as an already published October 2026 study. It is a relevant submission-time follow-up, not evidence that this project's contribution is first. [Publisher preview](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0043135426014624).

The defensible proposed contribution remains an **empirically evaluated integration**: topology-derived intervention rules with execution-level explanations, explicit flooding-first selection, all-four-outfall accounting, and storage-first behavior evaluated across observed and severity-anchored storms. The comparison must show whether learned control adds value beyond optimized rules, whether the shield improves safety beyond SAC-Lag, and whether any gain survives parent-level uncertainty and seed variability. Rule-trigger logs must reproduce executed interventions; feature importance alone does not establish this fidelity.

Before submission, complete the guide's structured evidence matrix from full papers: objective, safety mechanism, rainfall source, split unit, baselines, metrics, information available to each controller, seed counts and numerical validity. Repeat the search including Tian's knowledge-data fusion, safe/trustworthy RL and multi-RL/MPC work. Quantitative cross-paper percentages should remain contextual unless the same model, storms, control authority and metric definitions are used. Current hydraulic failures prevent any defensible claim that this project's results outperform these papers.
